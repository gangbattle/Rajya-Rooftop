About Rajya Rooftop

Located in the heart of Gachiya, Morang, Rajya Rooftop was built to redefine casual dining and café culture. Whether you are looking to unwind with a cup of authentic masala chiya, enjoy a vibrant evening with friends over fast food and cold beverages, or host special moments under the open sky, our rooftop space delivers a warm, welcoming ambiance paired with uncompromising flavor.
